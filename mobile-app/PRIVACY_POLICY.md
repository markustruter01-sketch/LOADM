# Privacy Policy for LOADMworkflow Mobile

*Last updated: June 16, 2025*

## Introduction

Digital Dynamics ("we," "our," or "us") operates the LOADMworkflow mobile application (the "Service") for military logistics training and aircraft load planning.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Service.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, military unit/squadron
- **Training Records**: Quiz scores, scenario completion data, progress tracking
- **Load Planning Data**: Aircraft configurations, cargo arrangements, balance calculations
- **Chat Messages**: In-app communications for training coordination

### Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, app version
- **Usage Analytics**: Screen views, feature usage, performance metrics
- **Error Logs**: Crash reports and diagnostic information for app improvement

## How We Use Your Information

### Training and Education
- Provide personalized training experiences
- Track learning progress and performance
- Generate performance analytics and reports
- Facilitate instructor-student communication

### Service Improvement
- Analyze app usage patterns to improve functionality
- Identify and fix technical issues
- Develop new features based on user needs
- Ensure app security and stability

### Communication
- Send important updates about training content
- Provide technical support and assistance
- Notify users of system maintenance or changes

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- Most data is stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite databases
- Sensitive information is protected using device-level encryption
- Authentication tokens are stored securely using iOS Keychain services

### Cloud Storage
- Training progress and submissions are synchronized with secure servers
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Servers are hosted in secure, compliant data centers

### Data Retention
- Training records are retained for educational and compliance purposes
- Chat messages are stored for 30 days locally, 90 days on servers
- Analytics data is anonymized and retained for service improvement

## Data Sharing

### No Sale of Personal Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

### Limited Sharing
Information may be shared only in these specific circumstances:
- **With Instructors**: Training progress and performance data within your organization
- **For Compliance**: When required by military regulations or legal obligations
- **Service Providers**: Trusted partners who assist in app functionality (under strict confidentiality agreements)

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- View your training records and progress within the app
- Update your profile information at any time
- Request deletion of your account and associated data

### Data Portability
- Export your training records and progress data
- Transfer data when changing military units or roles

### Opt-Out Options
- Disable analytics data collection in app settings
- Choose not to participate in optional surveys or feedback requests

## Children's Privacy

This app is designed for military personnel and is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

## International Data Transfers

Data may be processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated through:
